Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 9, 2026

This policy explains what information Ale Floors ("we", "us") collects through this website, how we use it and the choices you have. If you have any questions, contact us at alefloors10@gmail.com or +1 (347) 998-6695.

Information You Give Us

When you request a quote through our contact form, we collect the details you type in: your name, email address, phone number, city, the service you are interested in, the approximate area and your project description. If you contact us by phone, email or WhatsApp, we receive the information you choose to share in that conversation.

We use this information only to answer your request, prepare and send your quote, schedule visits and provide the services you hire us for.

Information Collected Automatically

Like most websites, our hosting provider records basic technical data when you visit, such as your IP address, browser type and the pages you request. This is used to keep the site secure and working.

We may use Google Analytics to understand how visitors use the site (for example, which pages are viewed and how people reach us). Google Analytics uses cookies and collects data such as approximate location, device and browser information. You can opt out with the Google Analytics opt-out browser add-on or by blocking cookies in your browser.

We do not use advertising cookies and we do not sell your personal information.

Third-Party Services We Use

  • Netlify hosts this website and processes the contact form submissions.
  • WhatsApp (Meta) — if you choose "Send via WhatsApp" or tap the WhatsApp button, your message is sent through WhatsApp and is subject to WhatsApp's privacy policy.
  • Google Maps displays our location on the About page. Google may collect data when the map loads.
  • Google Fonts provides the fonts used on this site; your browser requests them from Google's servers.
  • Google Analytics, when enabled, as described above.

How Long We Keep Your Information

We keep quote requests and project records for as long as needed to serve you and to meet our legal, tax and warranty obligations. You can ask us to delete your information at any time, unless we are required to keep it.

Your Choices

You can ask us to access, correct or delete the personal information we hold about you by emailing alefloors10@gmail.com.

Children's Privacy

This website is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The "Last updated" date at the top shows when it was last changed.
